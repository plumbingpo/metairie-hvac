How Baton Rouge Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a miserable night.
